  Decision    : allow robot-assisted injection only inside the validated limits above, with the surgeon in the loop
                (teleoperation, S6); any failed check -> stop, verify or redesign, never bypass a safeguard.
  People      : patient, surgeon, nurse/assistant, maintenance technician, hospital (owner of the device).
  Evidence    : S14 summary table + this register (simulation tests, calculations, cited limits).
  Responsibility: the surgeon approves each case; the design engineer owns the safety case and residual risks;
                the hospital owns maintenance and lockout procedures. Clinical use needs IEC 60601 / ISO 14971 approval.
  Perception  : the stereo camera is not a safety proof (like the AI case, slide 19) - docking is refused when the
                residual is large, and the surgeon confirms the entry point on the image.
  Privacy     : the camera sees the patient face - images are processed on-line, only the eye pose is logged.
